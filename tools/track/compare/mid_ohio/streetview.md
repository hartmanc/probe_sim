# Mid-Ohio: Street View record (round 4, 26 Oct 2026)

Coverage check (`headless_capture.ts svcover --lat 40.6895 --lon -82.6355 --zoom 16`, local screenshot
`inventory/streetview_cover.jpg`): Google Street View lines run only on the public roads (Ross Rd, Texter Rd, Gass Rd)
and on the paddock and parking access roads inside the grounds (the south paddock loop, the roads by the Mid-Ohio
School and the LEC campground, the car parks). There is no Street View on the circuit or in the pit lane, and no photo
sphere is listed on the track.

No panorama was surveyed this round. What the paddock-road panoramas could add (building faces, the backs of the
pit garages) belongs to the scenery stage, checked against the 2025-2026 video first (source precedence). The
eye-level record of the circuit comes from the three videos (`inventory.md`).
