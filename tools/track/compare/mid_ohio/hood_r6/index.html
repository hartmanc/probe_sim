Mid-Ohio round 6: the driver's-eye hood sheet (build-track 1.10)

Each sheet puts the sim's hood camera on the local package (round 5: geometry and racing line only; no scenery, far terrain or trees yet) beside the N837 Lemons hood camera (Apr 2026) and the 2025 IndyCar 360 lap's ahead view (pace car roof) at the same station. Judge the road only: the edges, the width, where each kerb starts and ends, crests and dips, and the T4 bank. Each caption says what to look at. The sim car sits on the centre facing the lap. For T8 and T4 a second still shows it on the solver's racing line. The ± after each frame's station is its time map's error. N837 has a frame every 1 s, the 360 lap every 0.5 s, so a frame can sit 10-20 m from the sim still.

Overview

T8 evidence (the round-5 finding)

The solver opens T8 to R 88.0 m (the centre's tightest radius is R 51.0 m). A smooth line through the real cars' positions gives R 77.1 m. The real cars stay at the left edge over the blind crest (s 2454; yellow kerb at s 2451-2466), apex on the right kerb at about s 2510-2540, and exit to the left kerb (s 2565-2610). The solver's line crosses diagonally from the T7 apex, so it is 2-4.5 m right of the real cars at s 2440-2490. The race AI's own profile takes the T8 apex at 113.3 km/h. The N837 session best covers s 2481-2589 at about 119 km/h on average (from the Catalyst delta), so lap 149's 85 km/h minimum belongs to that lap, not to the circuit. Full numbers: round6.md section 2.

T4 entry (the autopilot's wheel-off)

Under braking at 0.85-0.88 g into T4, the line reaches its 1.0 m left margin at s 1855-1871. The autopilot drifts 0.259 m outward, so the left front wheel's centre comes within 0.02 m of the edge and the tyre is over it for 6 grid rows (290 steps, 0.29 s). Sonoma, Buttonwillow and Laguna, driven by the same command, keep their wheel centres at least 0.148 / 0.144 / 0.068 m inside. The 360 car brakes about 1-1.5 m from the left line. The proposal is in round6.md section 3 (not applied).
