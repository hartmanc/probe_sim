## The track data model
track.json: n = 3560 stations at 1.008 m spacing along the centreline; per station centre xyz, left xyz, right xyz; cross-section profile z at 7 lateral fractions. Edges are stored as world points, so the renderer draws exactly these polylines (track ribbon vertices and a 12 cm painted edge line inset 0.15 m). No smoothing happens at render time.

## How the edges were produced (scripts under tools/track/, read them if useful)
1. measure_paint.py: for every station and every Google z20 capture covering it, sample the image along the station normal (0.1 m steps), classify each sample road / not-road (asphalt brightness & saturation vs the local asphalt), walk outward from the logged racing line until the next 1.2 m holds under 35 % road; median over captures. Each capture is registered to the NAIP ortho by a rigid shift (paint_regs.json; shifts up to 1.3 m, median 0.3 m).
2. rebuild_from_paint.py: centreline moved toward the painted midpoint by a stiff penalised fit; edges = those painted edges re-expressed on the new normals; "median 9, Gaussian 2 m, slope <= 0.25 m per station"; UNMEASURED stations keep the old NAIP-derived edge.
3. fix_edge_waves.py: per side, runs shorter than 25 stations departing > 0.35 m from a 41-station running median are replaced by the median; Gaussian sigma 5 stations; slope limit 0.10 m per station.
4. fix_bridge_edges.py: under 4 bridges (shadow), edges linearly interpolated across +-35 stations.
5. fix_edges_paint.py: 13 hand-chosen stretches (e.g. 'T2_entry_right' right 380-450 feature 'line') re-traced with a sweep-style detector along the normal; each stretch is spliced in with smoothstep blends at both ends, at least 12 stations long and lengthened until the blend's steepest step is <= 0.07 m per station.
6. Later today: fix_t11_exit_right.py (green band detection, sigma 5, blends), fix_rahal_left.py (target = crossing with the deployed polyline + sweep detections, 21-station median, sigma 6, blends lengthened until step <= 0.10 m), fix_edge_shape.py (monotone cubic through hand-measured knots), fix_front_straight*.py (constant gap to the walls).
7. edge_gate.py prints, per side, the rms of the half-width about its 41-station running median over stations with centre radius > 250 m (currently ~0.2-0.3 m), the max adjacent half-width step, cross-section steps, wall gaps, bridge windows. There is no threshold that fails on waves longer than ~40 m, and 0.10 m per station (a 5.7 degree kink of the edge relative to the road) was accepted as a "smooth" step.

## My diagnosis
(a) The source is a per-station classifier on 12 cm imagery with mostly one capture per station, 17 % holes, unmasked map labels/arrows/watermark, feature kind flipping (line / kerb / apron / shadow), and 0.3-1.3 m registration differences between captures; (b) the smoothing in the pipeline (median 9, Gaussian 2 m, later sigma 5) only removes wavelengths under ~15 m, so 20-80 m waves of 0.3-1 m survive, and "slope <= 0.10-0.25 m per station" permits kinks of 6-14 degrees; (c) holes and stretch boundaries add jogs and S-bends (every local fix blends into an unverified neighbour); (d) the gate measured the wrong wavelengths and had no acceptance threshold; (e) real painted lines are string-line smooth (design alignments: straights, spirals, arcs; width changes are linear tapers), so the right model is a low-parameter smooth curve, not a trace.

## My proposed fix
Replace tracing with fitting: per side, ONE penalised smoothing spline (or B-spline with knots every ~20-30 m) around the whole lap, fitted with a robust loss (Huber/Cauchy) to kind-consistent detections from ALL captures at once (white line inner edge, or kerb outer limit where a kerb exists, per an explicit feature table), with per-capture registration offsets (dx, dy) solved jointly so the curve is continuous across capture boundaries, map labels / arrows / watermark masked, holes bridged by the spline, no stitching or blends anywhere; curvature penalty set so the edge heading relative to the centreline changes <= ~0.3 deg/m except at listed features. Acceptance: residual of each half-width vs a sigma 15-25 station smooth <= 0.10 m rms and 0.25 m max on R > 150 m; kink <= 0.5 deg/station outside the feature list; plus a hood-camera lap sheet (48 frames) judged by eye.
