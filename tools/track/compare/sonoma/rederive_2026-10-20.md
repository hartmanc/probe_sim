# Sonoma re-derivation, first run (20 Oct 2026)

The test of `tools/track/compare/REDERIVE.md`: a fresh Opus agent (no memory of the Sonoma rounds) re-derived Sonoma
from the clean config (setup commit 6659bc5 on branch `rederive-sonoma`, from d8e5c5b) with only the skill, in
2 h 39 min of agent time; the orchestrator (Claude) audited its transcript, ran the comparison and judged the sheets.
Candidate: `rederive-sonoma` at d9273ca, registry rev 23d923f867. Reference: the accepted package on `track-pipeline`
(rev 3fa66ae8a4, round 7f). Tables and sheets: `rederive_2026-10-20/` beside this file (`compare_packages.md` and
`.json` committed; `sheets/` and `walk/` local).

## Verdict

The skill as written does not yet produce the accepted level in one pass. The candidate is recognisably Sonoma at the
structure level (grandstand, scoring tower, footbridge, pit buildings, board walls, 28 of 30 kerbs) and misses the
mid-scale evidence work of rounds 5-7: 261 of the 375 reference objects matched within 15 m, 114 missing, 23 extra, 24
height differences. The misses group into three scans the agent did not run to the end (3.4 barriers, 3.8 stations and
furniture, 3.9 boards and ground logos), one skipped step it reported (the 3.9 whole-lap logo scan), and a judge round
that was the builder judging itself. Every stage-3 gate passes on the candidate. The geometry was not reproduced, for a
pipeline reason (below), not an agent one.

## Stage 1 reads stage-3 keys (pipeline finding)

Stage 1's centre refit takes the pit-lane parts, the barriers and the pit-exit cone line from `build_scenery`
(`build_track.py` stage_geometry: `BS.pit_lane`, `BS.barriers`, `BS.pit_exit_cones`; config `pit_lane`,
`scenery.declared_walls`, `scenery.pit_exit_cones`), and `config_id('geometry')` leaves `scenery` and `pit_lane` out of
the hash. The orchestrator confirmed it on the accepted branch: `--stage geometry` with the FULL accepted config,
the same geometry hash 83d61a023d7b6ae0, the same captures and lidar (inputs hash 605053cde48d4fa6) and an identical
heightfield gives a different track.json: 4101.9 m (accepted 4098.8 m), `fit left: observations within 0.25 m` 89.5 %
(gate 90) and `fit right: waviness` 0.044 m at 2752-2814 (gate 0.04). The accepted geometry was built on 19 Oct at
18:05 with the round-3 scenery keys; the pit lane walls and the pit-out line declared in rounds 5-7e moved the pit
merge, and the periodic refit carried it round the lap. Consequences:
- fixed on branch pit-merge: stage 1 takes the merge from `centre.pit_exit` (stage-1 evidence) and reads no scenery key;
- the accepted Sonoma geometry rebuilds byte for byte (track.json 9d7d52be96485e08, every stage-1 gate passing);
- "byte for byte" is a reachable bar again for a re-derivation that measures the same wall end;
- the "frozen keys" rule is gone from the skill; moving Sonoma's merge to the round-7e evidence is the owner's decision.
