12. Round-3 preview (0da269d), on the unused 2020 bypass run-up to T10 (closed road, paved zone): it renders as a bare paved

    lines on the sides. It's usable track, just not in use" (owner_12_closed_road_look.png). Applies to every closed road.
13. Owner (same frame as 12 attached): "The walls at and following T10 are much higher, which gives the track a more
    claustrophobic feel." And overall: "The sim course resembles Sonoma, but it doesn't feel like Sonoma."
14. The frame for note 13: T10 entry (about s 3150-3230), hood view. The outside of T10 on the LEFT is open dirt with trees
    in the sim; the real corner has a tall concrete wall with a catch fence on the outside, continuing to T11 and the front
    straight (owner_14_T10_outside.png).

    dark panels) runs between the pit-in lane and the circuit, a double white line and a cone line along it, and at the
    wall's nose a yellow water/impact barrier before the concrete starts. owner_16 (sim, same spot): the pit-in lane is
    open to the track; the only wall is the far pit-lane wall. Owner: "There should be a concrete wall that separates pit
    in vs. the rest of the race track, with cones and a water/impact barrier before the concrete."

    tyre bundles and a red/white striped end block on the right, "Pit In" painted on the road, concrete apron. Owner: "Our

    (owner_20_pitin_buildings_streetview.png).
21. Street View "Turn 11" (reference only): a continuous tyre wall (dark tyres) along the outside of the hairpin in front
    of the concrete wall and fence; free-standing stacks of yellow / blue tyres, 3-4 high, at the inside apron edge of the
    hairpin and along the painted apron; the apron itself painted. Owner: "Need to add these tire barriers to T11 (they
    shouldn't be rigid; the tires should scatter if you run into the mound)" (owner_21_T11_tyres_streetview.png).
22. Street View from the pit lane (2017, reference only): the main grandstand at the line. Open bleacher seating rising

    "Likewise, the building agent should model the actual grand stands. That will make this feel like Sonoma"
    (owner_22_main_grandstand_streetview.png).
